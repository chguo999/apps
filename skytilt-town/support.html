How do I play?

Place a building on the tray and confirm the preview. Merge matching buildings to upgrade them. Each move shifts the tray’s balance. Fill your harbor order and keep the tray steady to dock. Drag to look around the board.

What if the tray tips?

Use Undo to rethink your last move, or restart the harbor. Open How to Play from Home or Pause for a refresher.

Where are my progress and settings saved?

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. Clearing app data may remove your progress. You can choose English or Chinese in Settings.
