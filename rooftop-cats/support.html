How do I play?

Choose a lot, choose a house height and tap Place house. Match the visible cat counts from all four streets. Taller or equally tall houses hide the houses behind them. Use the limited house inventory and fixed houses to solve each puzzle. How to play is available from Home and Pause.

What if I get stuck?

There is no countdown failure. Undo, remove or swap houses, keep candidate notes, restart a street or use a reasoning hint. Tap a street clue to inspect it at eye level.

Progress and decorations

There are 60 puzzles in three chapters. First clears earn coins; decoration groups unlock after 20, 40 and 60 completed streets. Decorations do not alter puzzle rules.

Language and sound

Settings contains the English/Chinese language selector, music, sound and reduced motion controls.

Optional advertising

Settings → Advertising preferences lets you choose whether to enable ads. You can play every puzzle without ads. On iOS, this version also requires system tracking permission before starting its advertising SDK. If permission is denied, advertising stays unavailable. You can review the permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad availability also depends on the network and providers.

Local saves

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save. Removing the app or its data may remove your progress; contact support before deleting it when troubleshooting.
