How to play

Pull the yellow parcel pod back to aim, then release. Use ramps and bumpers to guide it into the receiving ring. The parcel must settle inside the ring to complete a delivery.

Progress and settings

Progress and preferences are stored on the device. Deleting the app may remove them. See our Privacy Policy.
