How do I play?

Choose a new floor direction to tilt the vault and move the gems. Match three or more gems along any axis. Clear all the gold-rimmed seals to finish a puzzle. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, or inspect a slice of the board.

What if I run out of moves?

Undo a tilt or restart the puzzle. Each attempt includes one hint. Open How to Play from Home or Pause for a refresher.

Where are my progress and settings saved?

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. Clearing app data may remove your progress. You can choose English or Chinese in Settings.
