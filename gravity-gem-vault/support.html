How do I play?

Choose a new floor direction to tilt the vault and move the gems. Match three or more gems along any axis. Clear all the gold-rimmed seals to finish a puzzle. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, or inspect a slice of the board.

What if I run out of moves?

Undo a tilt or restart the puzzle. The first hint in each attempt is free. Open How to Play from Home or Pause for a refresher.

Optional ads and extra help on iOS

Version 1.0.0 has no ads. Version 1.0.1 offers up to two additional hints per attempt and one chance to add two moves after running out. The same limited help is available for free when optional ads are off or cannot initialize. Using extra moves rules out a master medal for that attempt.

In version 1.0.1, you can enable or disable optional ads in the game’s Settings. Enabling ads requests Apple’s tracking permission. If an ad is unavailable or closes before a reward is confirmed, you can still undo or retry for free. For details, see the privacy policy.

Where are my progress and settings saved?

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. Clearing app data may remove your progress. You can choose English or Chinese in Settings. The game has no account or in-app purchases.
