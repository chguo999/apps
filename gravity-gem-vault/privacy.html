Gravity Gem Vault iOS Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026. Chen Guo provides Gravity Gem Vault for iOS. This policy describes the data practices of versions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1.

Game progress

Puzzle progress, collection unlocks, and preferences stay on your device. The base game works offline and has no account, cloud saves, or in-app purchases. Deleting the app or clearing its local data can remove your progress.

Version 1.0.0

Version 1.0.0 has no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK.

Version 1.0.1

Version 1.0.1 includes optional advertising through TopOn and Smartdigimkt. Ads are off by default. The app initializes advertising only after you enable optional ads in the game’s Settings and grant permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. The app requests non-personalized ads and configures TopOn to block reporting of Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

When advertising is enabled, providers may process other device identifiers such as IDFV, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and network information, ad requests, impressions and interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics. Providers use this information to deliver and measure ads, help prevent fraud, and operate or diagnose the advertising service. Advertising providers may share data with their advertising partners. See TopOn’s advertising privacy policy and its iOS SDK data disclosures.

To stop future ad requests from the game, turn off optional ads in the game’s Settings. You can also withdraw tracking permission in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. The same limited gameplay bonuses are available for free if ads are off or cannot initialize.

Support and privacy pages

When you open our support or privacy page, GitHub Pages serves it over the internet. GitHub may process routine web request information under its privacy statement. Opening these pages is optional.

Support emails

If you contact us, we use your email address, message, and any attachments to respond to your request. We retain the correspondence as needed to resolve the issue and meet applicable obligations. Email services handle delivery.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about your support correspondence, email Chen Guo at 43905701@qq.com. Visit Gravity Gem Vault Support for gameplay help.

重力晶矿 iOS 隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 16 日。本游戏由 Chen Guo 提供。本政策说明 1.0.0 与 1.0.1 的数据处理方式。

关卡进度、收藏解锁和偏好保存在当前设备。基础游戏可离线游玩，没有账号、云存档或应用内购买。删除应用或清除本地数据可能会移除进度。

1.0.0 版

此版本没有广告、跟踪或分析 SDK。

1.0.1 版

此版本由 TopOn 和 Smartdigimkt 提供可选广告，默认关闭。只有在游戏设置中开启广告并通过 Apple 的“App 跟踪透明度”提示授权后，应用才会初始化广告服务。应用请求非个性化广告，并配置 TopOn 阻止上报 Apple 广告标识符（IDFA）。

开启后，广告提供方可能处理 IDFV 等其他设备标识符、IP 地址及据此推算的粗略位置、设备和网络信息、广告请求、展示和互动记录，以及崩溃或性能诊断数据，用于投放和衡量广告、协助防范欺诈、运行和诊断广告服务。服务提供方还可能向其广告合作伙伴共享数据。详见 TopOn 广告隐私政策及其 iOS SDK 数据说明。

在游戏设置中关闭可选广告，即可停止游戏后续广告请求。也可在 iOS 的“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪”中撤回跟踪权限。关闭广告或广告无法初始化时，相同次数的游戏辅助可免费领取。
