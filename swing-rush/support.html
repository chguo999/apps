How do I play?

Press and hold to grab the highlighted rope anchor. Release to fly, then hold again to catch the next anchor when it is in range. Land on the finish platform from above.

What if I miss a platform?

Tap Retry to practice the timing and try the course again. Open How to Play from Home or Pause for a refresher.

Where are my progress and settings saved?

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. Clearing app data may remove your progress. You can choose English or Chinese in Settings.

Optional ads and bonus coins

Version 1.0.1 adds Optional ads in Settings, off by default. The first four courses have no ads. From course 5, the first-clear result can offer one extra coin, once per course. The same capped bonus is free when ads are off or cannot initialize. An unavailable or interrupted ad does not count as a completed video. You can turn ads off at any time.

Interstitials can appear only when returning Home or to the course list after a successful course, at least three clears and 180 seconds apart. They do not interrupt a run, a retry, or the Next button. See the Privacy Policy for advertising data and choices.
