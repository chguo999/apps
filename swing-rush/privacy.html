Swing Rush iOS Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026. This policy covers Swing Rush for iOS. Version 1.0.0 has no advertising SDK; version 1.0.1 adds optional advertising. Chen Guo provides the game.

Game progress

Course progress, medals, unlocked colors, and preferences stay on your device. The base game works offline and does not require an account or cloud saves. Deleting the app or clearing its local data can remove your progress.

Optional advertising in version 1.0.1

Ads are off by default. If you enable Optional ads in Settings and authorize Apple tracking permission, TopOn and Smartdigimkt may process device identifiers, IP-derived coarse location and advertising interactions for third-party advertising and measurement. Performance information is used to operate and diagnose the service. Advertising data may be linked to your device and used for tracking across services; performance data is declared separately as not linked to your identity or used for tracking.

The integration requests non-personalized ads and blocks IDFA reporting. Permission is requested before advertising initialization. Network partners may process other device and network information, so this does not mean the advertising service collects no data. See TopOn's privacy policy for its practices.

You may refuse permission, turn Optional ads off in Settings, or revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings. Turning ads off stops new ad requests; it does not delete information already processed by partners. The game remains playable, and the same limited coin bonus is available without ads when ads are off or cannot initialize. Tracking permission does not increase the bonus.

Support and privacy pages

When you open our support or privacy page, GitHub Pages serves it over the internet. GitHub may process routine web request information under its privacy statement. Opening these pages is optional.

Support emails

If you contact us, we use your email address, message, and any attachments to respond to your request. We retain the correspondence as needed to resolve the issue and meet applicable obligations. Email services handle delivery.
