How do I match candies?

Drag to rotate the cube, or use the rotation buttons. Select two candies with the same symbol. Both must have a completely clear path out along a cube axis. Clear every pair to finish the level. You can reopen How to play from Home or Pause.

What if I get stuck?

There is no countdown. Undo a move, use the available hint, or reshuffle the board. Restarting a level resets that level’s current score.

Language and sound

Open Settings from Home or Pause to choose English or Chinese, adjust music and sound effects, or reduce motion.

Progress and device changes

Progress and preferences are stored locally. There is no in-game account or developer-operated cloud-save service. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove your progress. Contact support before deleting the app when troubleshooting; we cannot promise to recover deleted local saves.
