Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Chen Guo develops Cube Treats. This policy describes the current iOS version 1.0.0 (build 1) and these support pages. It does not describe the Android version, whose advertising integrations may differ.

Information stored by the game

The game stores puzzle progress, scores, coins, hint inventory, unlocked collectibles, display-base choices, and settings locally on your device so you can continue playing. The current iOS app does not require an account and has no chat or user-generated content features.

Collection, advertising and tracking

The current iOS build does not integrate an advertising SDK or a third-party analytics SDK. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay saves, and the current app does not send those saves to us. It does not use advertising identifiers for tracking. If these practices change, we will update this policy and any applicable disclosures before releasing the change.

When you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain relevant support correspondence. Email is processed by the email service providers involved in delivery. Please avoid sending information that is unnecessary for your request.

This website

These pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies set by us. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Its handling of this hosting information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Support correspondence is used for support and may be processed by service providers necessary to deliver email, or disclosed where required by applicable law. We retain correspondence only as needed for support, resolving related issues, and applicable legal obligations. Local game data remains on your device until removed by you or the operating system.

Your choices

You can change game settings within the app. Removing the app and its local data can delete saved progress; operating-system backups are controlled through your device and account settings. You may email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you provided through support. We will handle requests subject to applicable law and necessary identity verification.

Children

The current iOS game does not ask players to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us so we can review and address it.

Security and changes

We take reasonable care with support information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes to the app’s data practices will be reflected in the applicable app disclosures.
