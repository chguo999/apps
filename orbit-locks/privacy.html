Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026. Chen Guo develops Orbit Locks. This policy covers the iOS 1.0.0 version being prepared for release and these support pages. Android integrations may differ.

Game information stored on your device

Puzzle progress, ring states, unlocked levels, settings and processed advertising reward identifiers are stored locally. The game does not require an account and has no chat or user-generated content. We do not operate a server receiving your gameplay saves.

Optional advertising

The iOS app integrates TopOn and Smartdigimkt (TopOn ADX) for optional rewarded ads. These services may process device identifiers, device and network characteristics, IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Their SDK privacy manifests declare device identifiers for third-party advertising and tracking; TopOn also declares performance information. Requesting non-personalized ads does not mean no data is processed.

This build requests non-personalized advertising and disables TopOn IDFA upload. It starts the advertising SDK only after you choose Enable optional ads in Settings and iOS grants tracking permission. Declining either keeps the SDK from starting and does not block ordinary gameplay. Tracking permission can be reviewed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Turning off ads in the game stops new app-initiated ad requests. Restart the app to stop an SDK already initialized in that session. Disabling ads does not erase information already sent. Advertising services and their partners handle shared data under their policies, including international processing where applicable. See the TopOn Advertising Services Privacy Policy for processing, partners, retention and privacy-request information. Contact us if you need help directing a request to a provider.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments. We use these to respond and investigate your request; the email services involved process delivery. Send only information needed for support. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve issues and meet applicable obligations, rather than for a fixed promised period.

This website

We add no analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies to these pages. GitHub Pages hosts them and may record access information for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices and requests

You can decline optional ads, change settings and remove local app data. Removing data may erase progress; device backups are controlled through your operating-system settings. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support information and to exercise applicable privacy rights. Requests are subject to necessary identity checks and applicable legal obligations. Provider-held advertising information is handled under the provider policies; we cannot promise immediate deletion of all third-party records.

Age and security

This iOS release is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If a child has provided information through support, contact us. We take reasonable care with support information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.
