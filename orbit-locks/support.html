How do I play?

Drag an enamel ring to rotate it, then release to try an exit through the fixed guides. Drag the background to inspect the mechanism. Release all rings along clear paths to open the pearl star. How to play is available from Home and Pause.

What if I get stuck?

Undo a move, restart the puzzle, focus on a selected ring or reset the view. A free hint identifies a ring that can currently be released. An extra hint may be available through an optional rewarded video. There is no countdown or energy system.

Levels and progress

There are 18 handcrafted spatial puzzles. Solving a puzzle unlocks the next one; unlocked levels can be revisited. Your progress is stored on this device.

Language and sound

Settings contains the English/Chinese language selector, music, sound and reduced motion controls.

Optional advertising

Settings lets you choose whether to enable ads. You can play every puzzle without ads. On iOS, this version also requires system tracking permission before starting its advertising SDK. If permission is denied, advertising stays unavailable. You can review the permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad availability also depends on the network and providers.

Local saves

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save. Removing the app or its data may remove your progress; contact support before deleting it when troubleshooting.
